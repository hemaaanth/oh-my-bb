# Third-party components

## Webhook icon

`icons/webhook.svg` is the `WebhookIcon` glyph from
[Hugeicons](https://hugeicons.com) (`@hugeicons/core-free-icons` 4.1.3), MIT.
BB draws the same Hugeicons set, but its icon names have no webhook glyph.

## BB shared UI

`components/`, `lib/`, and `hooks/` come from the BB plugin registry
(`@bb` in `components.json`, pinned to `desktop-v0.46.0`), MIT, copied
unchanged with `npx shadcn add`. Rerun it to update them.
